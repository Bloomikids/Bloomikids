✨ Our Founder's Story

Built by an Educator.
Inspired by a Little Boy.
Made for Every Child.

BloomiKids didn't start in a boardroom. It started in a living room — with a curious toddler, a pandemic, and a mom who knew exactly what was missing.

🎓
Chapter 01 · The Foundation

With a background in Human Development & Education and a minor in Psychology, I entered the classroom with a deep understanding of how young minds grow — not just academically, but emotionally and physically. As a PreK & Kindergarten teacher, I saw children arriving at Kindergarten already behind on the foundations they needed to thrive.

“By Kindergarten, some children were already missing the basics — and I knew the earlier we reached them, the better.”

— Founder, BloomiKids
🍎
Chapter 02 · Going Earlier

That led me to move to Pre-Kindergarten — meeting children at the very start of their journey. Teaching 3 and 4 year olds reinforced everything I believed: nurture curiosity and movement early, and every foundation grows stronger. My career grew into a Daycare Director role, where I finally saw the full picture: what children needed, what parents struggled to find, and what every existing app was missing.

💛
Chapter 03 · The Turning Point — 2021

In 2021, at the height of COVID-19, I gave birth to my son. For the first time, the child I was educating was my own. I searched for apps that could build foundational skills, help my very active little boy slow down and be intentional, and incorporate the mindful movement I knew made a real difference. I could not find a single thing that brought it all together.

“I searched everywhere. I couldn't find anything that incorporated all these things — so I set out to create something my child and his friends would love to use every single day.”

— Founder, BloomiKids
Our Mission

BloomiKids exists to give every child — from PreK through 5th grade — a place where learning, movement, and mindfulness live together. Where screen time becomes grow time. Where every child has the chance to bloom.
