Our Founder's Story

Built by an Educator.
Inspired by a Little Boy.
Made for Every Child.

BloomiKids didn't start in a boardroom. It started in a living room — with a curious toddler, a pandemic, and a mom who knew exactly what was missing.

🎓
Chapter 01 · The Foundation

I've spent my whole career studying how children learn.

Armed with a degree in Human Development & Education and years in the classroom as a 🍎 PreK & Kindergarten Teacher, I watched children arrive at Kindergarten already behind on the foundations they needed. I knew the earlier we reached them, the better.

🍎
Chapter 02 · The Full Picture

I ran daycares. I finally saw what parents couldn't find.

Moving into 🏫 Daycare Director gave me the view no classroom does — what children need, what parents struggle to find, and what every existing app was missing. The answer was always the same: nothing brought it all together.

💛
Chapter 03 · The Turning Point

Then I had a son. And I built what he deserved.

In 2021, I gave birth during COVID-19. As a 💛 Mom & Founder, I searched for an app that could teach, calm, and move with my son every day. Nothing existed. So I built it myself — from the classroom floor to the living room floor.

Our Mission

BloomiKids exists to give every child a place where learning, movement, and mindfulness live together. Where screen time becomes grow time.

FAQ

Common questions

What ages is BloomiKids for?
PreK through Grade 5 — ages 3 to 11. Every activity is calibrated to the child's grade so it's always the right challenge, never too easy or too frustrating.
Is the yoga actually built in?
Yes — real pose photos, guided timers, and calming music. It's not a bolt-on feature. Movement and mindfulness are core to what BloomiKids is.
What happens after the free trial?
$8.99/month or $71.99/year. No credit card needed to start. Cancel anytime from your account — no calls, no friction.
How does the parent dashboard work?
A PIN-protected parent section shows time spent, skills practiced, accuracy, and progress — so you always know exactly what your child is doing and learning.
Can siblings share the app?
Yes — up to 5 child profiles per account, each with their own grade level, progress, and mascot. One subscription for the whole family.
Is it really completely ad-free?
Completely. No ads, no sponsored content, no third-party trackers. Ever. What your child sees is exactly what you signed up for — nothing more.
